Section 03

Two versions of ERP already happened. Neither asked the right question.

Version 1
~2007 · Desktop ERP

Schools adopted desktop software. It helped administrators — but students stayed disconnected, parents stayed disconnected, and teachers saw little benefit.

Version 2
Cloud ERP

Cloud made attendance, fees, homework, timetables, and results accessible everywhere. A real improvement — but most systems still only answer one question: "How do we digitize administration?"

The Next Question

Schools today need answers to bigger questions: How do we improve teaching? How do we improve learning? How do we prepare children for AI? How do we reduce teachers' workload? How do we engage parents?
